Straight answers.

How does the 10% work?+

It only counts sales I can prove came from the ads, using a discount code, a special link or the Meta pixel. You see the numbers in your monthly report before any invoice.

Who pays for the ads themselves?+

You pay Meta directly from your own ad account, usually £5 to £15 a day to start. My fee is separate.

Am I locked into a contract?+

No. It's month to month with 30 days' notice, and the website, domain and ad account all stay in your name.

What do you need from me?+

A 15-minute call, a few photos of your work and your OK on each ad. I handle the rest.

How soon will I see results?+

Your website is live in about two weeks and the ads start in week three. Ads usually need a few weeks of testing before they settle, and I'll tell you honestly how it's going every week.

I already have a website. Can you just run ads?+

Yes, if it works well on a phone. If it doesn't, I'll show you what's stopping people booking and you can decide whether to rebuild it.

What kind of businesses do you work with?+

Local businesses people book or buy from: barbers, salons, cafés, gyms, trades and shops, plus e-commerce brands selling online. If you're not sure, message me and I'll tell you straight.
