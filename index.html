FAQ

Straight answers.

Anything else, send me a message and I'll reply within one working day.

Why should I trust a new business?

Because you don't have to take anything on trust. Prices are on this page, everything is set up in your name, you approve every ad, there's no long contract, and the 10% only applies to sales you can see were tracked.

Do we ever need to meet in person?

No. Everything happens online through video calls, messages and email. You send photos and videos from your phone, and I do the rest.

Who pays for the ads?

You do, directly to Meta, from your own card. My fee is separate, so you always see exactly what Meta charged and you control the budget.

How is the 10% worked out?

Only sales tracked back to the ads count, through a discount code, a special link or the Meta pixel on your website. You see the numbers in your monthly report before any invoice.

Can I edit the website myself?

Small changes are included in the care plan, so you just message me. If you'd rather edit it yourself, I can build it on a platform like Wix or Squarespace instead.

How fast will I see results?

Ads usually need two to four weeks of testing to find what works, and most businesses get a clear picture after two to three months. You'll see the trend in every monthly report.

Do you guarantee sales?

No. Nobody can honestly guarantee that. What I guarantee is the agreed work, weekly checks on your ads and an honest report every month.

      Privacy policy
      
Your privacy, in plain English.

      
Last updated October 2026.

    

    

      
Who we are

      
Octave is a digital marketing studio run by Jesse Wilcock, based in Cumbria, United Kingdom. When this policy says "we" or "us", it means Octave.

      
What information we collect

      
We only collect what you choose to give us. That's usually your name, your business name, your phone number or email address, and anything you tell us about your business, when you request a call, fill in our contact form or message us.

      
How we use it

      
We use your details to reply to you, arrange and hold your call, and, if we work together, to deliver our services. We don't use your details for anything else without asking you first.

      
Who we share it with

      
We never sell your information. Messages you send us are handled through the apps you use to contact us, such as Instagram, TikTok or email, under those companies' own privacy policies. If we work together, we may use trusted tools (for example for invoicing) only as needed to provide our service.

      
How long we keep it

      
If we don't end up working together, we delete your details within 12 months. If you become a client, we keep records for as long as needed to provide our service and meet legal and tax requirements.

      
Cookies and tracking

      
This website does not currently use advertising or tracking cookies. If we add tools like the Meta Pixel in future, we'll update this policy and ask for your consent where required.

      
Your rights

      
You can ask us at any time to see the information we hold about you, correct it, or delete it. Just send us a message. If you're unhappy with how we've handled your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk.
